# DataMind Analysis Priority Rules

## Ranking Principles

1. High impact and high urgency findings come first.
2. High impact but slower chronic risks come second.
3. Medium impact and urgent findings come third.
4. Small impact or structurally healthy findings belong at the end or stay out of the summary.

## Concrete Rules

- If profit rate falls below the critical low threshold, rank it first regardless of scale.
- If the aggregate metric is stable but internal structure is drifting, mark it as a chronic risk and show the warning timeline.
- If a one-off event drives metric volatility, lower its priority after attribution and explain the level after excluding the event.
- If growth is high and profit rate is healthy, treat it as a positive signal near the end of the conclusion.

## Forbidden Conclusion Patterns

- Do not say "needs attention"; provide a concrete decision or next check.
- Do not treat event-driven shocks as structural problems.
- Do not rank all findings equally; there must be a clear first priority when multiple findings exist.
- Do not infer organizational causes unless supporting columns exist in the dataset.
